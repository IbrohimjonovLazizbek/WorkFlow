Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · workflow.myclass.uz

1. Local Guest Mode vs. Cloud Cabinet

You can use TaskFlow completely offline in Local Guest Mode without creating an account. In Guest Mode, your tasks, routines, reflections, and timer state stay strictly in your browser's localStorage and never leave your device.

2. Google Sign-In Data We Access

When you choose Continue with Google, TaskFlow requests only minimal identity scopes (openid, email, profile):

  • Your Google user ID, email address, display name, and profile photo URL to create your isolated Personal Cabinet.
  • A signed HttpOnly session cookie (tf_session) so your requests remain authenticated.
  • TaskFlow does not read your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, or Contacts.

3. What Cloud Data Is Stored

Your Personal Cabinet data is stored in an isolated Cloudflare D1 database partitioned by your unique user ID:

  • Tasks, subtasks, notes, completion comments, priorities, scheduled times, and focus time logged.
  • Daily routines, reflection entries (mood ratings, intentions, notes), and active focus timer state for cross-device sync.
  • Workspace preferences (theme, daily goals, timezone, reminder schedule).

4. Optional Telegram Integration

If you choose to link @myown_workflowbot inside Personal Cabinet, we store your Telegram chat ID and username solely to send your requested agendas, scheduled task reminders, focus completion alerts, weekly Sunday scorecards, and interactive updates for your own tasks. You can tap Unlink inside Personal Cabinet at any time to stop all Telegram messages immediately.

5. Export & Permanent Account Deletion

Your data belongs exclusively to you. You never share data with other users, advertisers, or third-party brokers.

Self-serve export & deletion: Export all tasks and settings anytime as JSON or CSV in Settings. To permanently erase everything from our servers, open Personal Cabinet → Google Account & Sync → Delete Account & All Cloud Data. All tasks, routines, reflections, timer records, settings, Telegram pairing state, and user profile rows are deleted immediately from Cloudflare D1.
